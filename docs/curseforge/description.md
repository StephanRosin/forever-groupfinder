# Forever Group Finder

A group finder built for **WoW: Forever**. One window replaces Blizzard's group finder: search groups and players, post your own listing, and get a group together without leaving it. Written from scratch, in the look of the other Forever addons. English, Deutsch, Español, Français.

## Two layouts
- **List and details** (default): the list on one side, the selected group or player with all details on the other.
- **Sidebar**: a slim list with the details beside it. Switch in the options.

## Activities
- Pick a category with the radio buttons; every activity shows a **fit colour** for your level.
- **Only in my level range** hides the rest.

## Groups
- Sectioned **per dungeon**, each section collapsible.
- Role icons in the order Tank, Healer, DPS — **free slots included** — plus leader, levels and group size.

## Players
- A big role icon with level, name, location, abbreviated dungeon tags, comment and playstyle.
- Sort by **role**, **level** or **newest**.

## Filters
- **Room for my roles** (on by default), **level fits**, **hide seen**, **language**, **playstyle** and a **text search**.

## My listing
- One table for your listing: status, roles (limited to what your class can do, with icons), playstyle, what it is for and a comment.
- One button to **Post**, **Update** or **Remove**.
- Warns before the listing **expires** and lets you extend it.
- Comments on your own listing need a **Blizzard account authenticator** — that is a Blizzard rule, not the addon's. Without one, the rest of the listing works as usual.

## Details and contact
- Members and free slots, **whisper** (with your own **whisper templates**), mark as **seen**, **hide**, **ignore** — with undo.

## Leader help
- Shows the **missing roles**, lists **fitting players** and invites them with **one click**.

## Searching
- A search runs **on every click** (the Search button, a category, a filter change). The game blocks addons from searching on a timer, so the list never refreshes by itself; it tells you when it is **outdated** with a gentle pulse.
- Rows update live while you look at them.

## Options
- Its own options window in the Forever style (`/fgf`, the minimap button or the addon compartment): **List look** with font and icon sizes, button sizes, layout, **Profiles** shared across characters, **export and import** as text.

## Commands
`/fgf` toggle the window · `/fgf options` · `/fgf search` · `/fgf lock` / `unlock` · `/fgf reset` · `/fgf ignore <name>` · `/fgf unignore <name>`
